Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 62.560.355 MARIA FERNANDA VIEIRA SIMOES KAC ("Kac Studio," "we," "our") collects, uses and protects your personal information when you visit our website or interact with our services. We operate in accordance with the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD — Law 13.709/2018).

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide voluntarily when contacting us, filling out forms or placing an order. This may include:

  • Name and surname
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping address (when applicable)
  • Content of messages you send us

We may also collect technical information automatically, such as IP address, browser type, pages visited and time of visit, through cookies and standard analytics tools.

2. How we use your information

Your information is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support
  • Process and fulfill orders
  • Send order updates and service-related communications
  • Improve our website and the quality of our service
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Data may only be shared with:

  • Shipping and logistics partners, strictly to deliver your order

  • Public authorities, when required by law or court order

4. Data storage and security

We adopt reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration or disclosure. Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel and used only for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Cookies

Our website may use cookies to improve browsing experience and gather anonymous usage statistics. You can configure your browser to block or notify you about cookies, though some website features may not work properly without them.

6. Your rights

Under the LGPD, you have the right to:

  • Confirm the existence of processing of your data
  • Access your data
  • Correct incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data
  • Request anonymization, blocking or deletion
  • Request data portability
  • Revoke your consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at mseccattocontabilidade@gmail.com.

We retain your personal data only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, or as required by applicable law.
