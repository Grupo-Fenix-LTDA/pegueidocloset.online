About

A quiet approach to fashion.

Founded in 2025 by Maria Fernanda Vieira Simões Kac in Curitiba, Kac Studio was born from a simple idea: fewer, better pieces. We work with a small circle of Brazilian ateliers and independent labels to bring together a wardrobe built on quality, fit, and character.

Our collection focuses on everyday elegance — clean silhouettes, honest fabrics, and accessories designed to travel between seasons. We ship across Brazil directly from our studio in Jardim Social.
